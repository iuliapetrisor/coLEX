# PayRo Terms and Conditions

Status: active · Effective: 2025-01-01

## 1. Who we are
PayRo SRL is a payment institution authorised and supervised by the National Bank of Romania.

## 2. Your account
You can hold balances in RON and EUR. We identify you before opening an account and store your name, CNP and IBAN.

## 3. Sending money
You authorise each transfer with a one-time password sent by SMS. We execute a transfer to the IBAN you give us. We do not check that the name of the payee matches the IBAN: you are responsible for entering the correct IBAN.

## 4. Transfers between your own accounts
Transfers between your own PayRo accounts are executed immediately and without additional checks.

## 5. Fees
Fees are listed in the Fee Schedule. An instant credit transfer in euro costs more than a standard credit transfer in euro because it is executed within seconds.

## 6. Complaints
Write to support@payro.example. We answer within 15 business days.
