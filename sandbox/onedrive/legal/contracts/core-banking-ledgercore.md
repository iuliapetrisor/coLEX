The Provider operates the core ledger for PayRo payment accounts. Subcontracting requires PayRo's consent.

## 2. Locations of data processing

## 3. Data protection
The Provider ensures availability, authenticity, integrity and confidentiality of ledger data, including personal data.

## 4. Access, recovery and return of data
PayRo can access, recover and obtain the return of all ledger data in an easily accessible format, including on insolvency of the Provider.

## 5. Service levels
Service level: 99.9% availability with quantitative performance targets, reviewed every year.

## 6. Assistance in ICT incidents
The Provider assists PayRo at no additional cost during any ICT incident affecting the ledger.

## 8. Termination
