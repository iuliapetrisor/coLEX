# SMS and One-Time Password Delivery Agreement

Parties: PayRo SRL and SMSNord Telecom SRL
Contract ID: CTR-2022-007
Status: active · Effective: 2022-06-15 · Renewal: 2026-06-15
Services: delivery of one-time passwords used to authorise transfers
Supports a critical or important function: yes
Subcontractors: Baltic Carrier Hub OÜ

## 1. Services and functions
The Provider delivers SMS one-time passwords for PayRo customers. Subcontracting is permitted with notice to PayRo.

## 2. Locations of data processing
Messages are processed in Romania and Estonia. The Provider notifies PayRo before changing a location.

## 3. Data protection
The Provider protects the availability, authenticity, integrity and confidentiality of message data, including phone numbers.

## 4. Access, recovery and return of data
On termination or insolvency PayRo can access and recover delivery logs, returned in an easily accessible format.

## 5. Service levels
Service level: 99.5% delivery within 10 seconds, measured monthly, with precise quantitative targets and service credits.

## 6. Assistance in ICT incidents
The Provider assists PayRo during an ICT incident at a cost determined ex-ante in Annex 2.

## 7. Cooperation with competent authorities
The Provider cooperates fully with PayRo's competent authorities and resolution authorities.

## 8. Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days' notice.

## 9. Security awareness and training
Provider staff attend PayRo's ICT security awareness programmes and resilience training on request.

## 10. Notice and reporting obligations
The Provider reports any development that may affect the agreed service levels.

## 11. Business contingency and security measures
The Provider maintains and tests a business contingency plan and ICT security measures.

## 12. Threat-led penetration testing
The Provider participates in PayRo's threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) when in scope.

## 13. Transition
On termination the Provider keeps delivering messages for a transition period of 6 months so that PayRo can migrate to another provider.
