# Cloud Hosting Services Agreement

Parties: PayRo SRL and Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL
Contract ID: CTR-2023-001
Status: active · Effective: 2023-03-01 · Renewal: 2026-03-01
Services: cloud hosting of the production payment platform and document storage
Supports a critical or important function: yes
Subcontractors: none

## 1. Services and functions
The Provider hosts the PayRo payment platform. Subcontracting of a critical function requires PayRo's prior written consent.

## 2. Locations of data processing
Services are provided and data is processed and stored in Germany and Ireland. The Provider notifies PayRo in advance of any change of location.

## 3. Data protection
The Provider ensures the availability, authenticity, integrity and confidentiality of PayRo data, including personal data.

## 4. Access, recovery and return of data
On insolvency, discontinuation or termination, PayRo can access, recover and receive all its data in an easily accessible format.

## 5. Service levels
Service level: 99.95% monthly availability, with quantitative performance targets and service credits. Service levels are reviewed yearly.

## 6. Assistance in ICT incidents
The Provider assists PayRo at no additional cost when an ICT incident related to the services occurs.

## 7. Cooperation with competent authorities
The Provider fully cooperates with the competent authorities and resolution authorities of PayRo.

## 8. Termination
PayRo may terminate with 90 days' notice, or immediately on a material breach.

## 9. Security awareness and training
Provider staff take part in PayRo's ICT security awareness programmes and digital operational resilience training.

## 10. Notice and reporting obligations
The Provider reports without delay any development that may materially affect its ability to meet the agreed service levels.

## 11. Business contingency and security measures
The Provider implements and tests business contingency plans and maintains ICT security measures, tools and policies.

## 12. Threat-led penetration testing
The Provider participates and fully cooperates in PayRo's threat-led penetration testing (TLPT).

## 13. Audit, access and inspection rights
PayRo, its appointed auditors and the competent authority have unrestricted rights of access, inspection and audit, including on-site, and may take copies of relevant documentation.

## 14. Exit strategy
On termination the Provider continues the services for a transition period of up to 12 months, allowing PayRo to migrate to another provider or in-house.
