# ICT Incident Procedure

Status: active · Last reviewed: 2024-03-10

## 1. Detection and classification
The on-call engineer records the incident in Jira and classifies it as minor or major within one business day.

## 2. Reporting to the National Bank of Romania
For a major incident the Head of Security sends an initial report within 24 hours of detecting the incident.
A final report is sent within 30 days of the initial report.

## 3. Communication
Customers are informed through the status page. Past incidents: see PAY-101.

## 4. Implementation
Deadlines are computed by `payments/incidents.py`.
